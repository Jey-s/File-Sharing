Private by design

  • Nothing is stored. Not on GitHub, not on any server. Your photos and videos are never uploaded. They exist only on your two devices, and only until you save them or close the page.
  • A direct, encrypted connection. Files travel straight from one device to the other, encrypted end to end. No server or relay sits in the middle of a transfer.
  • Pairing only. A free public service (PeerJS) briefly helps your devices find each other. It handles the pairing code and connection details, never your files, and public lookup servers only tell each device its own address. Each device learns the other’s IP address, and you see it before you allow a connection.
